**Ingredients:**

The High-Protein Plant Meat Sauce (Ragù)

Olive oil: 4 tablespoons

Yellow onions: 3 medium, finely diced

Carrots: 3 medium, finely diced

Celery stalks: 3, finely diced

Garlic cloves: 8, minced

Textured Vegetable Protein (TVP): 300g (dry weight, rehydrated in hot vegetable broth—massive protein source)

Brown or green lentils: 200g (dry, cooked) or two 400g cans, rinsed

Walnuts: 150g, finely chopped or pulsed in a food processor (adds meaty texture and protein)

Crushed tomatoes: 3.6 kg (four large cans)

Tomato paste: 6 tablespoons

Low-sodium vegetable broth: 1 liter (used for rehydrating TVP and thinning sauce)

Dried oregano, basil, and thyme: 2 tablespoons each

Soy sauce or tamari: 4 tablespoons (adds deep savory umami flavor)

Salt and freshly ground black pepper: To taste

2. The High-Protein Tofu-Cashew "Ricotta"

(Swapping dairy for blended firm tofu and soaked cashews/nutritional yeast)

Extra-firm tofu: 1.2 kg, pressed and crumbled

Raw cashews: 200g (soaked in boiling water for 30 minutes, then drained)

Nutritional yeast: 80g (adds a cheesy flavor and a big protein boost)

Lemon juice: 4 tablespoons

Garlic powder & onion powder: 1 tablespoon each

Salt and black pepper: To taste

Water or unsweetened soy milk: As needed to blend smooth

3. Assembly, Noodles & Plant-Based Cheese

Lasagna noodles: 24 to 30 sheets (standard or oven-ready, ensure they are egg-free)

Vegan mozzarella shreds: 800g (look for soy- or pea-protein-based varieties for extra protein)

Vegan parmesan shreds or alternative: 100g for topping

**👩‍🍳 Instructions**

Step 1: Prep the Plant Meats & Sauce

Rehydrate TVP: Pour hot vegetable broth over the dry TVP and let it sit for 10 minutes until plumped and liquid is absorbed.

Sauté aromatics: Heat olive oil in a massive stockpot or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add onions, carrots, and celery. Cook for 8-10 minutes until soft, then stir in minced garlic for 1 minute.

Build the ragù: Add the plumped TVP, cooked lentils, and chopped walnuts to the pot. Cook for 5 minutes, stirring often. Pour in soy sauce, crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, oregano, basil, thyme, salt, and pepper.

Simmer: Bring to a gentle boil, then reduce heat to low and let it simmer uncovered for 35–45 minutes so the flavors fuse and thicken.

Step 2: Make the High-Protein Tofu-Cashew "Ricotta"

Blend: In a high-powered food processor or blender, combine the pressed crumbled tofu, soaked cashews, nutritional yeast, lemon juice, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, pepper, and a small splash of water or soy milk.

Process: Blend until completely smooth and creamy, scraping down the sides as needed. Taste and adjust seasoning.

Step 3: Layer the Lasagna (Using Two 9x13-inch pans or one giant catering pan)

Preheat oven: Preheat your oven to 190°C (375°F).

First layer: Spread a thin layer of the plant-based meat sauce on the bottom of your baking pans to prevent sticking.

Noodles: Lay down a layer of lasagna noodles, overlapping slightly.

"Ricotta" layer: Spread a generous portion of the tofu-cashew mixture over the noodles.

Meat layer: Spoon a thick layer of the high-protein lentil-TVP ragù over the cheese.

Vegan Cheese: Sprinkle with a handful of vegan mozzarella shreds.

Repeat: Repeat the layers (noodles -> tofu-cashew mix -> ragù -> vegan cheese) until ingredients run out, finishing with a final layer of noodles topped with the remaining ragù and a heavy blanket of vegan mozzarella and parmesan alternative.

Step 4: Bake & Serve

Bake covered: Cover the pans tightly with aluminum foil (spray the underside with cooking oil so the vegan cheese doesn't stick) and bake for 35 minutes.

Bake uncovered: Remove the foil and bake for another 15–20 minutes, or until the vegan cheese is melted and bubbling at the edges.

Rest: Let the lasagnas rest for 20 minutes before slicing. Plant-based lasagnas need this resting time to firm up cleanly for serving!

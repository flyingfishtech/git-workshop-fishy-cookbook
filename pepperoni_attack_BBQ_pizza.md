**🛒 Ingredients & Albert Heijn Delft Shopping List**

1. The Mega-Dough Base (Sheet Pan Style)

AH Patent Tarwebloem (High-protein wheat flour): 1.2 kg (~€1.05)

Dry active yeast: 3 packets (~€1.20)

Olive oil (AH Olijfolie): 120 ml (~€1.50)

Sugar or Agave syrup: 2 tablespoons (~€0.30)

Lukewarm water: 700 ml

2. The Sauce & Plant-Based Meats Foundation

Sweet & Smokey BBQ Sauce (e.g., Remia / Sweet Baby Ray's): 450 ml bottle (~€3.99)

Plant-Based Pepperoni / Salami style slices (e.g., AH Vegetarische Salami or Garden Gourmet vegan pepperoni alternatives): 4 packs (approx. 400g total) (~€11.00)

Plant-Based Ground "Mince" or Soy Chunks (e.g., AH Vegetarisch Rulgehakt): 500g (~€4.50)

3. The Vegan Cheese Avalanche

Vegan Grated Cheese / Mozzarella Style (e.g., Violife Original Grated or AH Plantaardig Geraspt): 3 large packs (approx. 600g total) (~€9.50)

**👩‍🍳 Instructions**

Step 1: Build the Mega-Dough

Activate Yeast: In a large bowl, mix the lukewarm water, sugar, and active dry yeast. Let it sit for 5 minutes until it froths.

Knead: Add the 1.2 kg of AH Patent Tarwebloem and 60ml of olive oil. Knead vigorously by hand or with a heavy-duty mixer for 10 minutes until smooth and elastic.

First Rise: Cover the bowl with a damp towel and let it rise in a warm spot for 1.5 hours until doubled in size.

Step 2: Prepare the Plant-Based Meats & Pans

Cook the Mince: In a skillet with a splash of olive oil, sauté the plant-based minced meat with a dash of salt, pepper, and garlic powder until lightly browned and crispy.

Prep Baking Sheets: Generously oil two large standard oven baking sheets with the remaining olive oil. Split the risen dough in half and press/stretch each half evenly into the sheets, forming thick, pillowy crust edges.

Step 3: Assemble the Beasts

The Sauce Layer: Generously slather the smoky BBQ sauce across both sheet pan dough bases.

The Cheese Blanket: Scatter the vegan grated mozzarella heavily across the sauce.

The Plant-Meats: Distribute the seasoned vegan ground mince evenly over the cheese.

The Pepperoni Attack: Overlap the plant-based pepperoni slices aggressively across the entire surface so almost no cheese or sauce is visible underneath. Drizzle a final zig-zag of BBQ sauce over the top.

Step 4: Bake & Serve

Bake: Preheat your oven to 220°C (425°F). Bake the pizzas for 20 to 25 minutes until the crust is golden-brown and crisp, the vegan cheese is melted and bubbling, and the pepperoni edges are curled and dark.

Slice & Conquer: Slice each sheet pan into 8 massive student-sized squares (16 total slices). Serve hot to your hungry crowd!
